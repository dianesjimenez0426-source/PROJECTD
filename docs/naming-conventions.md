# Naming Conventions

Use lowercase and hyphens. No spaces.

## Client folders
`clients/client-name/`

## Design files
`client_YYYY-MM-DD_platform_format_topic_v#.ext`

Examples:
- `clientname_2026-12-05_ig_static_holiday-promo_v1.png`
- `clientname_2026-12-12_fb_carousel_services_v2.jpg`

When a design is approved, move it from `05-designs/drafts/` to `05-designs/final/` and drop the version number.

## Monthly files
`YYYY-MM.md` (e.g. `2026-12.md`) for content calendars, captions and reports.

## Large source files
Keep heavy working files (PSD, AI, video projects) in your cloud drive and link to them from the client README. GitHub works best with exported images and documents under 50 MB.
