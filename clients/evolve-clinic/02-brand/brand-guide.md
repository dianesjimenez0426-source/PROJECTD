- **Headlines:** dark teal with a short rule line
- **Footer:** teal bar with the address (2nd Floor, WP Fuel Inc. Building, White Plains, Quezon City) and 0999-220-2260
- **Photos:** large transparent cut-out model photos filling the right side down to the footer, with no empty space
- **Carousel closer:** doctor photo with the "Consult before you commit" CTA
